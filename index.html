What the app actually does

The same screens you use after onboarding: home, invoices, clients, items, and settings.

01

A finished invoice

Pick a client, add saved or custom items, set the due date, tax, and discount. The total is calculated for you.

02

Preview and share

Open a multi-page PDF, then share, print, or send it. Your logo and accent color carry onto the document.

03

Payment details

Bank details and a PayPal email show on the invoice when those fields are filled, and they follow the payment settings.

04

A clear home

Outstanding sits on the hero. Unpaid, overdue, and paid this month stay the same size. Total billed and collected sit underneath.

05

Clients and items

Keep people and prices once, then drop them onto the next invoice instead of typing them again.

06

Yours, on your devices

Choose an accent, follow light or dark appearance, and sync through iCloud when you are signed in.
