Help with InvoiceYOU

Set up the business, write an invoice, and send the PDF. If something is stuck, write to the same address used in the app.

Getting started

  1. Your business. Add the name, address, and logo during setup or in Settings.
  2. Payment methods. Turn on bank transfer or PayPal and fill in the details. They appear on the invoice when the field has text.
  3. A client and items. Save them once, then reuse them on the next invoice.
  4. Preview and share. Open the invoice and send the PDF, or print it.

Common questions

How do I create an invoice?
From Home or Invoices, start a new invoice, choose a client, add line items, then save. The preview opens so you can share the PDF.
Why don’t my bank details show?
Open Settings, then Payment methods. Fill in Bank details. The text is added to the payment section of the invoice and the PDF when that field is not empty.
How do I mark an invoice paid?
Open the invoice and mark it paid. Home then counts it toward collected and paid this month.
Can I change the look?
Settings includes appearance (system, light, or dark) and a set of accent colors. The accent is used in the app and on the PDF.
What is included before Pro?
You can create 3 invoices. Pro removes that limit. Yearly is $39.99 with a 3-day trial. Monthly is $3.99. Restore purchases from the subscription screen or Settings.
Where is my data?
Invoices, clients, and items are stored on the device and can sync with iCloud when you are signed in. You can export invoices as CSV or delete all data from Settings.
